### What I'm building

AI agents are starting to hire, pay and manage money for each other. None of them can check what another one really earned: records are self-reported, often the best of a thousand silent tries, and asking to see the model destroys its value.

**MIZAN is the check that runs before money moves.**

- **Check before you pay.** One call returns a signed ALLOW or REFUSE on what a counterparty really earned. Free, in milliseconds, fail closed.
- **Prove to get paid.** An agent proves its own record inside a zero-knowledge proof, after costs, with every try counted. It never reveals its model, code, prompts or strategy.
- **Don't take our word for it.** Every answer is signed, every verdict is anchored to Bitcoin, and the verifier re-runs any credential offline on macOS, Linux or Windows.

```
Registry      115 verdicts on the public registry · 87 refusals, published
Proven        392,877 market bars computed inside the proofs
Anchored      Bitcoin (OpenTimestamps) + RFC-3161
Proof         RISC Zero zkVM · STARK · verify in ~81 ms, offline, free
Standard      VTR-1, open and frozen: 41 requirements
For agents    MCP (check · prove · more) · A2A · x402 payment guard · Python + JS
```

**No capital moves to a machine without proof.**

### Why it will be trusted

I learned this problem the hard way. My own quant research system found backtests with Sharpe ratios above 10. I put real money on one: the backtest said it won almost every trade; live, it lost 7 of its first 8. The "edge" was a bug.

So I audited everything I'd built, **retracted 40+ of my own strategies with the root cause published on each**, and built the referee that would have caught them. I pointed it at my own work first. It refused most of it, including one of my own AI models at **+895%**. That refusal is [public](https://studio.mizan.market/credential/zkml11-verifiable-ai-nvda).

**A proof system that only shows winners is marketing. Trust comes from what it refuses.**
